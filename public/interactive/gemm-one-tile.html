One program, one tile of C

C = A @ B with M = N = 8, K = 12, and BLOCK_M = BLOCK_N = BLOCK_K = 4. Pick a program, then step along K and watch which pieces of A and B it loads.

Program (pid_m, pid_n):

B · K × N = 12 × 8

A · M × K = 8 × 12

C · M × N = 8 × 8

rows of A this program needs A chunk loaded this step columns of B it needs B chunk loaded this step its tile of C, in progress tile stored

Real kernels use the same structure at larger sizes, for example 128 × 128 tiles with BLOCK_K = 32. Click any square of C to pick the program that owns it.
