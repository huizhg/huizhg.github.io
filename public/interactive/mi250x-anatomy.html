Sources and how to read the numbers

AMD spec
A figure from AMD's MI200 datasheet.
docs
Official documentation from AMD, LUMI or Oak Ridge (Frontier).
measured
A published microbenchmark. The device is named when it is not an MI250X.
derived
Arithmetic on the figures above. The formula is shown under the value.
analysis
Independent architecture analysis (Chips and Cheese).

    Peak figures assume the 1.7 GHz peak engine clock. Latency comes from the MI250X where a measurement exists and from the MI210, a single CDNA 2 die with 104 CUs, otherwise. Diagrams are schematics, not floorplans.
